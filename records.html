How this works, and why it is accurate. Gmail never tells a sender where an email landed in someone else’s mailbox, so nobody can measure that for strangers. Instead, add a few of your own Gmail addresses as seed inboxes (sign in to each once). While sending, Efficient AI sends an identical copy to every seed at the start and every 25 emails, then opens each seed’s mailbox and reads the real label Gmail gave it: Spam, Inbox, or a tab. That is a true measurement for those mailboxes and a good sample of how your real emails land at Gmail. It cannot see other providers or your real recipients’ inboxes. Bounced is exact: it comes from the undeliverable notices in your own mailbox.
